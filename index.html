Ritual Privacy Policy

Last updated: 21 October 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Ritual, a habit-tracking application, handles information when you use the app.

1. Information We Handle

Depending on how you use Ritual, information may include your account details, such as your email address, and information you enter into the app, such as habits, completion records, and progress data.

2. How Information Is Used

Information is used to support account authentication, save and display your habits and progress, and provide the app's features. We aim to use information only for purposes relevant to operating and maintaining Ritual.

3. Storage and Third-Party Services

Ritual may use third-party infrastructure providers for authentication, database storage, hosting, and app functionality. These providers may process information as necessary to deliver their services. The providers and data flows used by the released app should be described accurately in this policy.

4. Sharing of Information

Personal information is not sold by the developer. Information may be processed by service providers supporting the app and disclosed where required by law. Any additional sharing must be disclosed here.

5. Data Retention and Deletion

Information may be retained for as long as necessary to provide the service and meet applicable obligations. You should be able to request deletion of your account and associated data using the account deletion option in the app, if available, or by contacting the developer. Applicable retention limits and deletion procedures should be followed.

6. Data Security

Reasonable safeguards are used to protect information. However, no electronic storage or transmission method can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

7. Children's Privacy

Ritual is not designed to knowingly collect personal information from children in violation of applicable law. If you believe a child has provided personal information improperly, contact the developer.

8. Your Choices and Rights

Depending on applicable law, you may have rights to access, correct, or request deletion of your personal information. You can contact the developer with questions or requests.

9. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated as Ritual's features or data practices change. The latest version will be published on this page with an updated date.

10. Contact

For privacy questions or data-related requests, contact the developer through the contact email listed on Ritual's Google Play Store listing.
