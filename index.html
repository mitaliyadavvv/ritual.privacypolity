Ritual Privacy Policy

Last updated: 21 October 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Ritual, a habit-tracking application, handles information when you use the app.

1. Information We Collect

Depending on how you use Ritual, information may include your email address and account information, along with information you enter into the app, such as habits, habit completion records, and progress data.

2. How We Use Your Information

Information is used to authenticate your account, save and display your habits and progress, and provide the app's features. Information is used for purposes related to operating, maintaining, and improving Ritual.

3. Data Storage and Third-Party Services

Ritual uses third-party infrastructure where configured to support authentication, data storage, hosting, and app functionality. These providers may process information as necessary to deliver their services.

The specific services and data practices used by the released version of Ritual should be reflected accurately in this policy.

4. Sharing of Information

We do not sell your personal information. Information may be processed by service providers that support Ritual or disclosed when required by applicable law. Any additional sharing of personal information will be disclosed as applicable.

5. Account and Data Deletion

You may request deletion of your Ritual account at any time by contacting us at mitaliyadavvv@gmail.com . Include your account email address so we can identify the account associated with your request.

If an account deletion feature is available within the app, you may also use that feature to initiate deletion. Account deletion is not subject to a mandatory waiting period imposed by this policy.

Account deletion is intended to remove your account and associated personal data, including saved habits and progress records, where applicable. Some information may need to be retained where required by law or for legitimate security purposes. Any such retention should be limited to what is necessary.

Requests will be handled as promptly as reasonably possible. Contact us if you have questions about the status of a deletion request.

6. Data Security

Reasonable safeguards are used to protect information. However, no method of electronic storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

7. Children's Privacy

Ritual is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children in violation of applicable law. If you believe a child has provided personal information improperly, contact us using the email below.

8. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on applicable law, you may have rights to request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information. Contact us to make a request.

9. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when Ritual's features or data practices change. The latest version will be published on this page with an updated date.
